### Mixed-state phases

Physical systems are never in isolation. We investigate many-body physics in open quantum systems and have uncovered new physical phenomena, such as a new type of [spontaneous symmetry breaking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03639), and [topological phases that can only appear in open systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02390). We have also discovered [quantum topological order at finite temperature in three dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02928).

### Defects and extended operators

Extended operators probe quantum many-body systems in ways local observables cannot. We study disorder operators, which measure the symmetry charge in a spatial region, and Rényi entanglement entropies of subsystems — both realized as defects in spacetime. Their scaling behavior reveals new universal quantities characterizing quantum phases.
